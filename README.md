# Flash

Flash is a fast-paced geography game that challenges how well you recognize real-world places in Singapore from a unique perspective.

Instead of photos or street view, you are dropped into a Google Maps-based 3D environment where only the building shapes and landmark structures are visible. Your goal is simple: identify the landmark before the timer runs out.

🎮 How it works

- You are shown a 3D map view of a hidden landmark
- You have 30 seconds to figure out what it is
- Type your answer into the input box
- You can skip if you’re unsure
- Each round contains 10 landmarks

Your speed and accuracy both matter — the faster you answer, the better your performance.

🗺️ Game modes

- Landmarks: Explore famous places around the world, including iconic buildings and tourist attractions.
- Regions: Test your knowledge of Singapore’s geography across the 5 regions
- Daily Challenge: Compete in a shared global challenge where everyone gets the same 10 landmarks each day. Compare scores on the leaderboard and see how you rank against other players worldwide.

🏆 Competitive leaderboard
Track your performance and compete with others in the Daily Challenge leaderboard

⚡ Features

- 3D map-based gameplay using real-world locations
- Time-based challenge system (30 seconds per question)
- Typing-based answer input
- Skip option for difficult questions
- 10-question game rounds
- Daily global challenge with leaderboard
- Smooth audio and visual feedback
- Clean, fast-paced UI designed for quick sessions
- 🧠 Train your spatial memory

FLASH is designed to test more than just knowledge — it challenges your ability to recognize places from structure, layout, and visual memory. Improve your geographic awareness while competing for the best times.

- Puzzle and quiz game fans
- Players who enjoy fast competitive challenges
